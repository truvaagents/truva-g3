What is Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)?

Human-in-the-Loop is a safety pattern where AI systems pause execution to request human approval before performing sensitive operations. In Truva-G3, HITL creates checkpoints that wait for human decisions (approve/reject) with configurable timeouts. This enables oversight of AI tool invocations at the plan level or individual step level.
